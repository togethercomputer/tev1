# Article charts

PNG images and editable SVG originals copied from the completed “Create
shareable results chart” task. The article embeds only that task's final two
charts, comparing the latest 2B and 4B v2.1 checkpoints.

## Current article charts

- `jev-vs-together-jev-v21-main`: main-test accuracy and task breakdown, with
  policy transfer omitted from the image. Evidence:
  [2B](../../evaluation/v2.1/README.md),
  [4B](../../evaluation/v2.1-4b/README.md), and
  [Jev](../../evaluation/v1/jev/report.json).
- `research-paper-classification-v21-no-glm`: agreement with model-judge
  consensus, with GLM omitted from the image. Evidence:
  [paper comparison](../../evaluation/papers-v2.1/README.md).

## Historical charts

These show the original $4 2B checkpoint and remain available for its archived
evaluation notes; they are no longer embedded in the article.

- `jev-vs-together-jev`: accuracy against dataset labels and executable policy
  rules. Evidence: [evaluation/v1](../../evaluation/v1/README.md).
- `research-paper-classification`: agreement with model-judge consensus on 891
  papers across 24 categories. Evidence:
  [evaluation/papers-v1](../../evaluation/papers-v1/README.md).

The charts represent different metrics and test populations. The paper score is
not human-verified accuracy. The article captions preserve that distinction.
